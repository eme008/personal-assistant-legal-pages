Privacy Policy

Mariel's Personal Assistant · Last updated: 10 November 2026

What this app is

Mariel's Personal Assistant is a private tool built and used only by Mariel to organise her own Google accounts. It is not offered to anyone else, and no other people's accounts are connected to it.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app reads and organises email in the owner's Gmail accounts (labels, filters, drafts) and reads and creates events in the owner's Google Calendar.

How the data is used

The data is used only to sort the owner's email, notify the owner about important messages, prepare and send replies on the owner's behalf, and manage the owner's calendar. Replies are sent only with the owner's approval or under rules the owner has explicitly set.

Storage and sharing

Data is processed on the owner's own computer and server. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account settings: Security → Third-party apps with account access.
